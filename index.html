Route accuracy & sources

All transportation is planned, not booked. Exact 2027 schedules, direct services, border arrangements and June 12 day-trip logistics are unconfirmed.

Rail legs with available geometry follow OpenStreetMap railway ways, connected by an inferred path through corridor stations. This is railway infrastructure, not proof of the exact route a booked train will take. Missing legs retain clearly labeled approximate connectors. Blue lines end at railway stations; numbered city markers remain at your city-center coordinates. Select a rail leg to see its stations and a Google Maps transit link. Intermediate places are routing points, not added stays.

The Bosnia drive and coastal return follow mapped roads from OpenStreetMap through OSRM. These are routing suggestions, not a booked tour. The Bosnia option goes via Trebinje and returns along the coast. Check entry requirements, border hours, rental-car permissions and feasibility before booking.

The tentative ferry is an illustrative sea connector into the Bay of Kotor, not evidence of an operating Dubrovnik–Kotor ferry. Both ferry directions are separate selectable legs. Do not assume this service exists.

Flight arcs are illustrative. U.S. connections use the requested Missoula–Seattle–London and Athens–Seattle–Missoula chains; they do not assert nonstop availability. City-center markers are not airport or station locations.

OpenStreetMap tile policy: attribution stays visible, only visible tiles are requested, no bulk downloads or prefetching, browser caching is honored. Standard OSM is enabled only on an HTTP(S) page with a web referrer. The downloaded file uses embedded outlines by default.

Backgrounds: OpenStreetMap contributors; Natural Earth (public domain); OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA); Esri World Imagery. Online satellite and terrain require internet and provider availability. They are not bundled for offline use.

Road routing: OSRM / FOSSGIS. Rail corridor references: Eurostar, DB, ÖBB. Map controls: Leaflet 1.9.4.
